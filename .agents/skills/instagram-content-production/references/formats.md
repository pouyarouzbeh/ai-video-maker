# Format guidance

These are production defaults. Adapt them to the request and inspect how the actual content crops at mobile size.

| Placement | Default composition | Delivery and review |
|---|---|---|
| Reel | 1080 × 1920 | MP4, cover and caption when relevant. Review with and without audio, plus a centered 4:5 feed crop of the cover. Check motion and subtitle timing. |
| Single image | 1080 × 1350 | JPG or PNG and caption. Review the hierarchy and text at phone size. |
| Carousel | 1080 × 1350 for each slide | Numbered JPG or PNG files and a caption. Keep aspect consistent, check slide order, and make the first slide understandable on its own. |
| Story | 1080 × 1920 per item | Ordered JPG/PNG and/or MP4. Keep essential text clear of top and bottom interface overlays. For native polls, links or stickers, deliver placement and text notes alongside the assets. |

For Persian content, check RTL shaping, mixed Latin/Persian text, font availability and line breaks. Use actual subject references where identity matters. Review source accuracy, legibility, visible crops and output order. For video, use the FFmpeg skill for the final technical pass.
