# Instagram portfolio reel

The primary agent directs and edits. Read the project brief and assign acquisition, motion, sound and review to the matching project agents. Lock the intended audience, narrative, and frame ranges before motion or music production. The shared handoff is brief.json for intent, assets.json for local files and their public sources, and timeline.json for cut boundaries and sound cues.

## Editorial decisions

- Establish a single subject in the first second, then show the actual website within three seconds. Make the value of the website apparent with a real task or interaction. Keep the ending still long enough to read the brand credit and call to action.
- Plan each shot as a subject, a camera or object action, and a readable hold. Use a matching silhouette, screen position or direction at the cut. Accelerate and settle, staggering secondary motion and shadows. Apply blur during fast moves and keep UI stable and sharp on the hold.
- Source original product media and capture real, loaded mobile interface states. Preserve photo proportions and color. Show actual gallery changes and scroll states. Keep perspective shallow during reading. Use photos on planes for parallax and Blender for geometric objects, lighting and a camera orbit.
- Use an offline Persian font, lang=fa, RTL direction, and enough width to preserve word shaping. Keep essential copy within the project's mobile overlay margins and test the central feed crop. Ensure the image tells the story muted.
- Keep Remotion motion deterministic in frames and read inputs from local files. Use Shotcraft cards for motion reference and the official Remotion skill for rendering practices. Render heavy shots serially on a laptop.
- Start with a complete low-resolution animatic and cue-aligned music. Use original or licensed audio. Sound events follow the exact frame of the on-screen action. Verify loudness and peak after AAC encoding.

## Review

View every cut and action at mobile size with and without sound. Verify text readability, clipping, exposure consistency, stable holds, source traceability and actionable CTA. Probe and decode the delivery file, checking exact frame count, portrait dimensions, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, yuv420p and faststart. Report any defect with its frame and correction. Keep the editable brief, assets manifest, timeline and shot sources together.
